# Experience Parsing & Relevance Engine

## 1. Objective

The Experience Parsing & Relevance Engine analyzes professional experience from resumes and determines how relevant previous roles are to a target job role.

## 2. Experience Parsing

The experience parser extracts:

- Company name
- Job title
- Start date
- End date
- Employment duration
- Total professional experience

The system supports date formats such as:

- January 2024
- June 2025
- 2024
- Present

## 3. Total Experience Calculation

The system calculates the total professional experience by adding the duration of all detected employment records.

The result is provided in:

- Total months
- Total years

Example:

```text
Total Experience: 22 months
Total Experience: 1.83 years
